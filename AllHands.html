04 / 10
New vocabulary nº 2

What is a skill.md file?

A small recipe for one repeatable job. Hover the steps.

explain_chart / skill.md
name: explain_chartdescription: Use when summarizing a dashboard chart.
Steps:1. State the main pattern first.2. Use the approved metric definition.3. Mention caveats and refresh timing.4. Avoid claiming cause without evidence.5. End with one useful next question.

Why it matters

Skills make good work repeatable — instead of depending on one person remembering the right process.

Team mindset

If your team repeats a task, that task may become a skill.

Write it once, review it, reuse it everywhere.

06 / 10
Raising the bar

What “good” looks like

The goal is connected work, not isolated demos.

Less of this

• One-off prompts
• Unclear definitions
• Teams rebuilding the same glossary
• Dashboards with hidden context
• No impact numbers

More of this

• Shared context
• Reusable data products
• Skill files for repeatable tasks
• Benchmarks and examples
• ROI and usage evidence

Why it matters

AI tools will be judged by usefulness.

Usefulness depends on our preparation, documentation, and review loops.

Every finished project should point to a number.
